## The fine print that voids your cash back

Cash back terms read like boring legal text right up until they cost you money. These are the rules that actually trip people up, pulled from the current Apple Store and Best Buy offer terms:

1. **Start with an empty cart.** Both Apple and Best Buy require it. Items already sitting in your cart from an earlier session can break attribution.
2. **Don't use coupon codes from outside the portal.** A code not displayed on TopCashback voids the cash back at Apple Store — even if the code itself works.
3. **Skip Safari.** TopCashback explicitly says it can't guarantee tracking on Safari for these retailers; use Chrome or another browser.
4. **Cash back is calculated on item price only** — no taxes, no shipping, no fees. A 2% rate on a $499 iPad is about $10, computed before the ~8% sales tax most states add.
5. **Apple enforces lifetime purchase limits** — six units per iPad model per account, along with similar caps on other categories. Buy past the limit and the cash back stops.
6. **Bulk and resale purchases are ineligible** and can be declined outright.

None of this is hard. It's just four or five clicks in the right order: portal first, empty cart, no outside codes, standard browser.
